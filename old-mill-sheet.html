Corner footing (engineer)

  1. Dig 500 × 500, 800 deep into the clay.
  2. Bitumen the buried part of the post.
  3. Hang the post plumb with its foot 150 off the bottom; prop both ways.
  4. Pour a 250 concrete pad: 150 under the post, post 100 in.
  5. Once set, backfill ≈550 of soil over the pad, rammed in layers. The soil gives the uplift hold-down.

Stumps: 400 × 400 pads, 200 deep, with the anchor cast in.

Studio-side holes A1, A2: centred on the post, a 500 hole would run about 100 under the studio. Dig them from the studio line outward so the post sits off-centre in the pad, and keep clear of the trailer stands.

Engineer's changes (Ollie Harris, 2 Nov 2026)

  • Bearer lines A and B: single 190 × 45 MGP10, continuous, inside face of the posts.
  • Bearer line C: double 190 × 45 MGP10, continuous, on 3 stumps.
  • All bearers continuous and supported by an intermediate post.
  • Joists continuous over the span, max 450 centres; keep the bigger size.
  • Diagonal timber knee brace at all 4 corner posts: min 120 × 45, M12 bolt each end.
  • Posts 100 × 100 in-ground hardwood cast straight in: 800 hole, 250 pad (150 under the post), ≈550 soil backfill. Stump pads 400 square × 200.
  • B1 and B2: 2/240 × 45 MGP12.
  • Rafters 190 × 45 at 930 and fascia 140 × 35: approved as drawn.
