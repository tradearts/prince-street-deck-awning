Bracket setting (engineer's detail)

  • Centre the bracket in the hole; post foot just above ground.
  • Orientation: bracket plates parallel to the studio wall (the bearer line), so the bolt runs towards the shed, the same way as the knee-brace bolts.
  • Plates plumb and square to the setout lines; prop them until the concrete sets.
  • Footings 500 × 500 × 500.
Before you pour: check the bracket's stem fits a 500 deep hole with concrete under the end. A 600 long high-wind anchor won't; if that's all that's available, ring Ollie before digging deeper (650 deep would take about 75 bags instead of 60).

Engineer's changes (Ollie Harris, 2 Nov 2026)

  • Bearer lines A and B: single 190 × 45 MGP10, continuous, inside face of the posts.
  • Bearer line C: double 190 × 45 MGP10, continuous, on 3 stumps.
  • All bearers continuous and supported by an intermediate post.
  • Joists continuous over the span, max 450 centres; keep the bigger size.
  • Diagonal timber knee brace at all 4 corner posts: min 120 × 45, M12 bolt each end.
  • Posts on cast-in brackets, oriented as drawn; footings 500 × 500 × 500.
  • B1 and B2: 2/240 × 45 MGP12.
  • Rafters 190 × 45 at 930 and fascia 140 × 35: approved as drawn.
