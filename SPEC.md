# Deck and awning: specification

Preliminary design for a treated-pine deck and awning between the studio and the shed. **The structural numbers are preliminary checks, not an engineer's design.** They're here for review.

## Key dimensions

- Deck 4 800 × 4 000, decking top 687 above ground. 42 rows of 90×22 decking, 20 overhang on every edge.
- Roof pitch 5.0°: high side 3 700 under the studio eave, low edge 3 219. Rafter length 5 521.
- Four 150×150 H4 posts at the deck corners, 4 650 apart along the deck and 3 760 between the studio-side and shed-side lines.
- Post lengths: studio side 4 341, shed side 4 012.
- Lowest beam (B2, deck edge) underside 2 177 above the deck; B1 2 560 above the deck.
- Joists 3 960 long, 13 of them at about 445 centres, on three bearer lines (studio-side posts, 2.0 m, shed-side posts).
- Fence-end screen: 43 vertical 70×45 slats to 2 165 above the deck, spanning 3 910 outside the posts.

## Footings and posts

- **Post ×4.** 150×150 H4 treated pine at the four deck corners, concreted 900 deep with 100 gravel under. Bitumen on the buried part to 150 above ground. Studio side 4 341 long (cut from 4.8 m), shed side 4 012 (cut from 4.2 m). About 70% used at N3 with no braces. _Source: BBBM or Old Mill · price to confirm_
- **Footing.** Shed-side posts 600 dia × 1 000, now about 14 kN of uplift each (was 19). Studio-side 450 dia × 1 000, about 6 kN each now that they carry part of the roof. Stumps 400 × 525. Dome the tops so water runs off. _Source: Bastion 20 kg premix · aisle 400 · about 115 bags, or a small truck load_
- **Stumps ×5.** 90×90 F7 H4 on Pryda High Wind 90 anchors, packed between the bearer plies: one mid-span on each post line and three on the middle line. _Source: Ballina aisle 407 · I/N 8422819 · $76.02 (one 2.7 m makes all five)_
- **Post anchor.** Pryda High Wind post anchor, 600 stem, M12 bolts through the post. 125 size for the posts, 90 size for the stumps. Rated about 36 kN uplift. _Source: Ballina aisle 6 bay 15 · 600×125 I/N 1120005 $31.25 · 600×90 I/N 1120004 $24.50_

## Deck

- **Deck bearers.** 190×45 H3 MGP10, 4.8 m, one ply each side of the posts and stumps on three lines: the studio-side posts, a stump line through the middle at 2.0 m, and the shed-side posts at the deck edge. About 60% used on the middle line. _Source: Ballina aisle 407 · $83.52 · 6 needed, 29 in stock_
- **Deck joists ×13.** 140×45 H3, 3 960 long in one piece, sitting on all three bearer lines at about 445 centres, so each span is about 1.8 m. Set 20 in from each edge so the decking covers the ends. The two edge joists stop at the posts. _Source: Ballina aisle 407 · 4.8 m (Ballina doesn’t sell 4.2) · $62.83 · 13 in stock, exactly what this needs_
- **Decking.** 90×22 H3 treated pine, 42 rows from 5.4 m lengths, about 5 mm gaps. Overhangs the joists by 20 on every edge to cover their ends, and is notched around the four posts. _Source: Ballina aisle 402 · I/N 8032593 · $24.30 · 236 in stock_

## Roof structure

- **B1 high beam.** 2/190×45 H3 MGP10, 6.0 m, either side of the studio-side posts. With the shorter cantilever it now takes about 30% of the roof, so it’s up from 140: about 77% used. Strap every rafter down to it. _Source: Ballina aisle 407 · I/N 8032322 · $104.40 each_
- **B2 beam, deck edge.** 4/240×45 H3 MGP10, 6.0 m, two plies each side of the shed-side posts at the deck edge. Now about 65% used (was 88%). It’s the lowest beam: 2 177 above the deck. _Source: Ballina aisle 407 · I/N 8032345 · $145.62 each_
- **Rafters ×6.** 190×45 H3 MGP10, 6.0 m, one over each post line and four between, at 930 centres. 3.76 m back span and 1.6 m cantilever toward the shed. About 46% used at N3. _Source: Ballina aisle 407 · I/N 8032322 · $104.40 · 19 in stock_
- **Purlins ×8 rows.** 70×35 H3 on flat, full width, 300 past the end rafters. Both the clear poly and the Zincalume screw into these. _Source: Ballina aisle 407 · I/N 8032115 · $30.66 each_
- **Fascia.** 140×35 H3 across the rafter ends to keep the cantilever tips in line. _Source: Ballina · I/N 8032253 · $60.30_
- **Clear corrugated poly.** About 3.0 m wide: 4 sheets × 5.6 m, lapped one corrugation over the Zincalume. Ordering later.
- **Your leftover Zincalume.** 6 × 3.2 m sheets as 3 runs at the fence end, each run lapped 200 over a purlin. _Source: Already on site_

## Fence-end screen

- **Vertical batten screen.** 43 vertical 70×45 H3 on edge (a 45 face with 45 gaps, as before, now standing up), outside the two fence-end posts on the dividing-fence side. They run 3.9 m from the outside of one post to the outside of the other, from about 160 below the deck edge up to 2 165 above the deck, so they also hide the deck framing. Two 2.3 m slats cut from each 4.8 m length. Screw each slat to both rails. _Source: Ballina aisle 407 · 70×45 H3 × 4.8 m · $31.58 · 68 in stock_
- **Screen rails.** Two horizontal 70×45 H3 rails the slats fix to. The bottom rail is screwed through a 35 mm packer into the deck’s edge joist every 600, so it’s supported its whole length. The top rail is held by the posts at each end and three short 70×45 hangers screwed to the side of the edge rafter above, about 1 m apart, so it never spans more than about 1 m. 15 mm packers where the rails cross the posts. _Source: Same 70×45 H3 × 4.8 m stock_
- **Heat panel noggins.** Two 70×45 noggins between the posts behind the BBQ to carry the FC heat panel, now that the battens have moved outside the posts. _Source: Same 70×45 H3 stock_
- **Heat panel.** 6 mm fibre cement on 25 mm spacers off the battens, 1.4 m wide behind the BBQ, from 50 above the deck to 2.0 m. Air can move behind it, and the timber isn’t right behind the hood. _Source: Ballina · HardieFlex 6 mm sheet, plus spacers · not in this price_
